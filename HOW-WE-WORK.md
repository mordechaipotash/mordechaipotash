## Why This Matters

The industry frames AI work as:
- "AI-assisted" — human does the work, AI helps
- "AI-generated" — AI does the work, human disclaims
- "Human in the loop" — human supervises, AI executes

All three dodge the question: **Who is responsible for the output?**

Our answer: **The human. Always.**

> "In reality that's exactly where the responsibility lies. No matter how good AI gets, it's always going to need the human trigger. Always."

Mordechai doesn't "use AI tools." He conducts an agent. The way a conductor is responsible for every note — not because they play the instruments, but because they chose the piece, set the tempo, shaped every phrase, and took the stage.

**The conductor takes the bow AND the blame.**
